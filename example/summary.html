Two processors, two philosophies of hiding latency

CPU · 18 cores

Hides latency with cleverness — out-of-order execution, branch prediction, prefetch. Fast even on one thread.

1.9 TFLOP/s  ·  300 GB/s
GPU · 40 cores

Hides latency with sheer numbers — ~5,000 lanes, thousands of threads. Needs ~250k threads to wake up; below that, slower than the CPU.

14.9 TFLOP/s  ·  578 GB/s
